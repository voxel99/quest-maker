The response is a concrete branching plan for the requested quest:
- a clear starting location and several named locations (at least one per suspect or clue area);
- choices/edges that connect locations, with no location left without an exit unless it is an ending;
- a gating mechanism for the hidden safe (a fact or item the player must obtain first);
- at least 2 distinct endings that depend on the player's choices (e.g. correct vs. false accusation);
- choices are meaningful, not cosmetic.
Fail if the answer is only generic advice with no concrete structure.
